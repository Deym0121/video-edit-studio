# Tutorial voiceover script

`Studio_Tutorial.mp4` (4:07) is narrated with these lines (AI voice: ElevenLabs “Nathaniel Hartley”), over a music bed. To use your own voice instead, record over `Studio_Tutorial_music_only.mp4` (the same video with music and clicks only): each line starts at the time shown, and the screen does what the line says while you say it.

Teleprompter / subtitle files: `voiceover_en.srt` (English) and `voiceover_tl.srt` (Taglish). After you change a line, re-record it (`vo/`), then rebuild with `../../.venv/bin/python build.py --render`; the timing follows the recorded lines.
